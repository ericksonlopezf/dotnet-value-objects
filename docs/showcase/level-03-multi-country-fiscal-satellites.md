# Level 03 — Multi-Country Fiscal Satellites

In Level 03, we explore fiscal identifier satellites for LATAM countries.

---

## 1. Supported Fiscal Satellites

- **Dominican Republic**: `Rnc`, `Cedula`, `Ncf` (`EricksonLopez.ValueObjects.Fiscal.DominicanRepublic`)
- **Chile**: `Rut` (`EricksonLopez.ValueObjects.Fiscal.Chile`)
- **Colombia**: `Nit` (`EricksonLopez.ValueObjects.Fiscal.Colombia`)
- **Mexico**: `Rfc`, `Curp` (`EricksonLopez.ValueObjects.Fiscal.Mexico`)
- **Peru**: `Ruc`, `Dni` (`EricksonLopez.ValueObjects.Fiscal.Peru`)
- **Argentina**: `Cuit`, `Cuil` (`EricksonLopez.ValueObjects.Fiscal.Argentina`)
