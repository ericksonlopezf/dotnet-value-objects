# Internationalization & Multi-Jurisdiction Strategy

> **Global Standards & Regional Tax Mandates**

---

## 1. Global Standards
- **Currency**: ISO 4217 uppercase 3-letter currency codes (`CurrencyCode`).
- **Country**: ISO 3166-1 alpha-2 2-letter country codes (`Country`).
- **Language**: ISO 639-1 language codes (`LanguageCode`).
- **Phone**: ITU-T E.164 international numbering plan (`PhoneNumber`).

---

## 2. Latin American Regulatory Jurisdictions
- 🇩🇴 **Dominican Republic**: DGII (RNC, Cedula, e-CF).
- 🇨🇱 **Chile**: SII (RUT, DTE Folio).
- 🇨🇴 **Colombia**: DIAN (NIT, CUFE, CUDE, CUNE).
- 🇲🇽 **Mexico**: SAT CFDI 4.0 (RFC, CURP, Fiscal UUID).
- 🇵🇪 **Peru**: SUNAT (RUC, CPE).
- 🇦🇷 **Argentina**: ARCA/AFIP (CUIT, CUIL, CBU, CVU, CAE).
