# Strategic Positioning & Ecosystem Placement

> **Positioning Statement & Target Developer Persona**

---

## 1. Positioning Statement

For enterprise software architects and .NET engineers building high-performance, compliant distributed systems and financial engines, **EricksonLopez.ValueObjects** is the premier Value Object and fiscal compliance suite. Unlike general-purpose wrapper libraries or ad-hoc validation logic, it combines zero-allocation `readonly record struct` performance, Martin Fowler lossless allocation, compile-time Roslyn analyzer safety, and official statutory tax satellites for Latin American jurisdictions.

---

## 2. Target Workloads & Personas

- **FinTech & Payment Processors**: Requiring high-precision `Money`, strict currency safety, and zero-cent-loss revenue splitting.
- **Enterprise ERP & Billing Engines**: Operating across Dominican Republic, Chile, Colombia, Mexico, Peru, and Argentina requiring statutory tax ID and e-invoice validation.
- **High-Throughput Microservices**: Requiring zero heap allocations on hot paths to minimize GC latency and memory footprint.
- **NativeAOT / Serverless Containers**: Requiring instant startup and 100% trim-safe execution.
